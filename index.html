About the book

Faith, leadership, and the workplace

HR Evangelist speaks to a tension many Christian professionals navigate: the perceived divide between the practical demands of human resources and the faith they profess as followers of Jesus Christ.

This book bridges that gap by placing people, leadership, and organizational culture under the Lord’s direction. It reminds us that leadership over people is not merely administrative or technical; it is a sacred trust exercised on God’s behalf.

Readers are challenged to lead by example, allowing Christ to be seen through character, integrity, discernment, justice, and excellence.

About the author

Meet Mwikali Muthiani

Mwikali Muthiani is a human resources and organizational development specialist with close to 20 years of work and consultancy experience with an international focus.

She oversees MillennialHR, a Nairobi-based HR consultancy firm founded in 2014. Her professional interests include human rights, people management, organizational development, leadership, and workplace culture.

Mwikali has served on international boards, including Amnesty International, Avaaz Foundation, World Animal Protection, Amref Health Africa, and Faulu Bank.

She co-authored Understanding the Pathology of INGOs, published in August 2023, and was a columnist for the Friday Daily Nation newspaper for eight years, offering weekly HR advice on well-researched topics.

Mwikali is also a mother, grandmother, and leader of the intercessory ministry at Calvary Worship Centre in Nairobi.
